Why Diffusion Samples Move Toward the Mean

A surprising high-noise behavior

By Alec Helbling

September 5, 2026

A curious property of diffusion models: when a sample is mostly noise, the model’s best guess is close to the mean.

So samples can initially be pulled toward the center of a dataset, even when no data exists there, before rebounding toward the data manifold.
